About

What Humanity Tomorrow is

Humanity Tomorrow is an educational project dedicated to explaining AI risks and life extension to a broad audience. The website features extensive FAQs on both topics, covering the most common arguments for and against, misconceptions, and questions.

Who writes it

The project was created and is developed by one person—Markel Kori (me). I work to communicate ideas about AI risks and safety, radical life extension, and science and rationality more broadly.

The website currently receives no funding and is maintained entirely by me. For me, this website is a way to make the world a little better.

Editorial principles

Every text on this website is written entirely by me, by hand. AI is used only for translation and gathering information.
